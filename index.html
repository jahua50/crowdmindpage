01 Vision

To be the most trusted brand image partner for businesses, helping Taiwan's best companies shine, online and in the real world.

Rooted in Hualien

Grounded between the mountains and the Pacific, we understand the warmth and grit of local businesses. Starting from Hualien, we set out to prove that great technology and design know no geographic limits.

Online meets offline

Website, social media, search results, storefront signage, print. Wherever customers meet your brand, they should feel the same professionalism and quality.

Facing the world

With Taiwan as our starting point, we aim to bring this approach to brand image across Asia-Pacific, helping more businesses step confidently onto the international stage.

02 Mission

Technology that lowers the barrier.
Design that raises the value.

Many businesses offer excellent products and services, yet lose opportunities at first impression because their online presence is incomplete or their visuals are inconsistent. Our mission is to turn professional-grade brand image capabilities into tools every business can easily own.

04 Our Journey

Where we've been so far.

  1. Dec 2024

    CrowdMind is founded

    CrowdMind Technology Co., Ltd. is incorporated in Hualien, Taiwan (Tax ID 97614829), with a mission to make professional brand image tools accessible to every business.

  2. 2025

    PixelTrend is built

    We develop PixelTrend, our brand image and visual trend insight tool, to help businesses keep their image consistent and current across channels.

  3. 2026

    PixWeb launches

    PixWeb launches, giving businesses fast, responsive corporate websites that serve as a professional online storefront.

05 Roadmap

Step by step, toward a bigger stage.

  1. Phase 1 · Deepen

    Refine our products, serve every client well

    Continue improving PixelTrend and PixWeb, starting with businesses in Hualien and eastern Taiwan and expanding to SMEs nationwide, building a repeatable method for brand image.

  2. Phase 2 · Integrate

    Connect online and physical channels

    Extend from websites and social media to storefronts, exhibitions and print, offering an all-in-one omnichannel image solution and a partner ecosystem of local designers, printers and marketers.

  3. Phase 3 · Intelligence

    AI-driven brand image management

    Bring in AI to automatically check brand consistency across channels and anticipate visual trends, so businesses keep a more professional image with less effort.

  4. Phase 4 · Go global

    From Taiwan to Asia-Pacific

    Help Taiwanese businesses reach overseas markets with a world-class image, while bringing CrowdMind's products and services to the Asia-Pacific region.
